Notes on how this was put together

Journeys are traced from the homepage outward: for each page, the report asks Google Analytics which pages sessions opened next. Every branch traces as far as its own traffic supports. Smaller paths are grouped into "Other pages" so no single chart becomes a wall of tiny entries — the same rollup Google Analytics uses for its own long tail. "Other pages" is a group rather than one page, so it isn't linked or clickable.
