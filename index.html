Counts are sessions — one visit by one person, however many pages they open in it. The figure at the trunk is every session on the site in the period, and the branches beneath it are the pages those sessions started on. A page marked ↗ opens the live page in a new tab.

Notes on how this was put together

Sessions are grouped first by the page they started on, which is why the total matches Google Analytics' own session count rather than only counting homepage arrivals. From each of those pages the report asks Google Analytics which pages sessions opened next, and traces onward branch by branch as far as each branch's own traffic supports. Smaller paths are grouped into "Other pages" so no branch becomes a wall of tiny entries — the same rollup Google Analytics uses for its own long tail. "Other pages" is a group rather than one page, so it isn't linked or expandable.
